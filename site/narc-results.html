NARC Comprehension Results

Can a vision model actually read a NARC puzzle — every frame, every dimension, every colour — before we ask whether it can solve one? Each run is scored three ways: overall cell accuracy, shape (did it see where things are) and colour (did it see what colour they are).

How responses fail structurally

Separate from whether the grids were right: was the response even well-formed? "Clean" means it returned exactly the frames asked for, at the right dimensions, in order. "Hallucinated masked frame" means it invented the frame it was explicitly told to skip.

What makes a puzzle hard

Week 3 found that "dense, many-colour puzzles collapse almost entirely" on MARC. These test the same claim on NARC: reconstruction accuracy against grid area and against how many distinct colours a puzzle uses.
